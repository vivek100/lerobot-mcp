02 / The agent's toolbox

Tools that turn intent into action

The agent reads the guide, looks at the scene, makes a bounded move, then checks what actually happened. The same tools work in simulation and on the physical arm.

Understand the scene

robot_guide
Learn forward, left, up, camera conventions, limits and recovery.
observe
See measured pose, remaining room, real photos and simulated views.
detect_target
Find the largest matching color or face and show its image bounding box.
list_animations
Discover gestures, held states and speech moods.
show_wrist_camera
Open a read-only live camera viewer in an MCP Apps-compatible host, with this web page as fallback.

Move, follow and verify

move
Move the gripper in centimetres; adjust tilt and roll.
set_gripper
Open or close the jaws without moving the arm.
track
Follow a visible color or face for a bounded time.
run_animation
Wave, nod, hold a thinking pose or speak.
stop
Cancel the active motion and hold.
go_rest
Lift and fold the arm into its rest pose.

Every action comes with evidence

01

Read the guide

Shared words and units, fixed to the robot base.

02

See the scene

Physical photos show objects. Model previews explain arm posture.

03

Act locally

The local runtime applies joint, speed, table and contact guards.

04

Verify the result

Read the outcome and inspect the next image. Closure alone does not prove a grasp.

Real-camera boxes are image coordinates. Metric projection needs calibration; the physical camera cone is illustrative. Wrist tracking centers the image; fixed-front tracking only approximates horizontal pointing.

Live camera viewing does not move the robot. The viewer's internal frame-refresh tool is hidden from agents in compatible MCP Apps hosts.
